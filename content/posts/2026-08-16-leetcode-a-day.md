Most people use Leetcode as a way to ensure their ticket for a job, others use it as exercise for 
their youths, but recently i have found a different perspective on leetcode.

It does not feel like Leetcode is so much of a burden, no, it's more like a habit for enlightenment.
Of course, saying this, i am not trynig to convey that i am an expert of DSA or something, for a number of problems, i still have to look it up on concepts and spend long hours just to solve them
But, to be honest, it feels a whole lot more fun doing leetcode now, compared to the past where it feels like a laborious task to complete.

In all honesty, practicing and solving it, as well as watching guides like Neetcode, is a unique way to practice Logical Thinking, like video games (which i am trying to avoid nowadays).
