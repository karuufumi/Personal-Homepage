## The premise

Static deployment is a good default for a portfolio: fast, low-maintenance, and easy to reason about.

## Working through it

A small server-rendered site keeps the source portable and the operational surface clear. The PHP entry point and public assets can be deployed together without a separate frontend build step.

## Current takeaway

The practical lesson is to avoid operational machinery until the portfolio actually needs it.
